About CUBIX IMPRINT

Your trusted partner for books, printing supplies, and professional printing services since 2010

Our Story

Founded in 2010 in the heart of Sivakasi, CUBIX IMPRINT began as a small family-owned bookstore with a simple mission: to make quality books accessible to everyone in our community. What started as a passion project by book lovers has evolved into a comprehensive hub for literature and printing services.

Over the years, we've expanded our offerings to include a wide range of printing supplies and professional printing services, while never losing sight of our core identity as champions of knowledge and learning. Today, we serve thousands of customers, from individual book enthusiasts to businesses requiring high-quality printing solutions.

Our journey has been marked by continuous growth, technological advancement, and an unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction. We've embraced digital transformation while maintaining the personal touch that has always defined our service.
